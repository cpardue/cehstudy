CEH v13 Module 9: Social Engineering — Free Practice Questions with Explanations

Question 1: Phishing conducted over a telephone call is called:

Answer: B — Vishing = voice phishing; smishing = SMS; quishing = QR codes; whaling targets C-suite executives.

Question 2: Pretexting involves:

Answer: C — The attacker researches the target and builds a believable cover story; baiting (USB) and tailgating are separate techniques.

Question 3: Spear phishing differs from mass phishing because it:

Answer: C — Personalization (names, projects, recent events) gathered via OSINT dramatically raises click-through and credential-harvest success.

Question 4: A 'whaling' attack targets:

Answer: B — Big fish — CEOs, CFOs — have authority to approve wire transfers and access the most sensitive data.

Question 5: Following an authorized person into a restricted area is called:

Answer: C — Carrying boxes or acting in a hurry makes it seem the follower belongs — a physical social-engineering staple.

Question 6: Waterholing is when attackers:

Answer: A — By contaminating trusted destinations, the attacker reaches many victims at once without individual contact.

Question 7: The 'authority' principle in social engineering exploits people's tendency to:

Answer: C — Other levers include scarcity/urgency, familiarity/liking, consistency, and reciprocity — all used to override caution.

Question 8: Phishing via lookalike domains such as microsof.com or company.com.co is an example of:

Answer: A — Typosquatted or extra-TLD domains mimic the real brand to fool users into entering credentials on fake login pages.

Question 9: The single most important defense against social engineering is:

Answer: C — Technology can't stop a willing click — trained people who verify requests, simulate-tested regularly, are the real control (plus MFA to limit damage).

Question 10: QR-code phishing ('quishing') works well because:

Answer: B — Victims never see the URL before scanning — a parking ticket, event pass, or Wi-Fi sign can point anywhere.
