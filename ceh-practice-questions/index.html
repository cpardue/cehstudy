Frequently Asked Questions About CEH Practice Questions

How many free CEH practice questions does CEHStudy offer?

CEHStudy's question bank contains 200 original multiple-choice CEH v13 practice questions — 10 per module across all 20 exam modules. Every question includes a detailed explanation of the correct answer and why the other options are wrong.

Are these real CEH exam questions?

No. These are original practice questions created by CEHStudy that follow the style, difficulty level, and domain coverage of the actual CEH v13 exam. They are not leaked or reproduced EC-Council exam questions. Use them alongside official EC-Council study materials for the most complete exam preparation experience.

What is the CEH v13 exam format?

The CEH v13 exam (exam code 312-50) consists of 125 multiple choice questions to be completed within 4 hours. You need a score of at least 60% (75 correct answers) to pass. Questions are drawn from all exam domains, with Scanning Vulnerabilities the heaviest-weighted domain at 20%.

How are the practice questions organized?

By module, matching the CEH v13 curriculum: footprinting and reconnaissance, scanning, enumeration, vulnerability analysis, system hacking, malware, sniffing, social engineering, DoS, session hijacking, IDS/firewall evasion, web servers, web applications, SQL injection, wireless, mobile/IoT, lateral movement, post-exploitation/IR, and cryptography. How many practice questions do I need before taking the CEH exam?

Most successful candidates complete between 500 and 1,000 total practice questions across all domains. Work through all 200 questions in our free bank twice — once by module, once mixed — and focus on the explanations for anything you missed.
