Frequently Asked Questions About CEH Study Plans

How long does it take to prepare for the CEH exam?

Most candidates spend 4-8 weeks preparing. The 30-day intensive plan requires 4-6 hours daily and suits those with security experience. The 6-week standard plan at 10-15 hours weekly works for beginners and working professionals.

Can I pass CEH with just these free study plans?

The study plans are designed to be used alongside our free flashcards (306 cards across 20 modules) and practice questions (200+ questions with explanations). Hands-on lab practice is recommended but not strictly required to pass the multiple-choice exam.

Which study plan is right for me — 30-day or 6-week?

Choose the 30-day intensive if you have 4+ hours daily and prior security experience (Security+, CISSP, or hands-on IT). Choose the 6-week plan if you can study 10-15 hours weekly or need to build foundational knowledge first.
