CEH vs Other Cybersecurity Certifications

How does CEH compare to other popular cybersecurity certifications? CEH vs CompTIA Security+ — CEH focuses on offensive security and penetration testing while Security+ covers broader cybersecurity fundamentals and is often a prerequisite for entry-level roles. CEH vs CISSP — CISSP is management-focused covering security architecture and policy while CEH is hands-on technical ethical hacking. CEH vs PenTest+ — both cover penetration testing but CEH has broader domain coverage including IoT OT cloud computing and mobile hacking. Choose the certification that matches your career goals.
