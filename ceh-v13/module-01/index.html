CEH v13 Module 1: Getting Familiar with Basic Information Security

This module introduces the foundational concepts of information security. You'll learn about the CIA triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability), security controls, risk management frameworks like NIST, and the defense-in-depth approach that underpins all ethical hacking activities.

Key Topics Covered

  • CIA Triad: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability — the core principles of information security
  • Security controls: technical, administrative, and physical controls
  • Risk management: risk assessment, risk mitigation, risk acceptance
  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover
  • Defense in depth: multiple layers of security controls
  • Authentication factors: something you know, have, are

Important Terms & Concepts

CIA Triad: The three pillars of information security — Confidentiality (only authorized access), Integrity (data hasn't been altered), Availability (systems/data are accessible when needed).
Defense in Depth: A strategy of implementing multiple layers of security controls so that if one fails, others still provide protection. Like layers of an onion.
Risk Management: The process of identifying, assessing, and prioritizing risks followed by coordinated efforts to minimize, monitor, and control the probability or impact of unfortunate events.

How to Study This Module

  • Understand each principle of the CIA triad with real-world examples
  • Know the difference between preventive, detective, and corrective controls
  • Memorize the NIST Cybersecurity Framework phases

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most important principle in security?
All three CIA principles are equally important, but confidentiality is often prioritized first since data breaches have the most immediate legal and reputational consequences.

What is defense in depth?
A security strategy using multiple layers of protection. If one layer fails, others still provide defense. Examples: firewall + IDS + endpoint protection + access controls.
