CEH v13 Module 19: Attacks on OT and ICS

This module covers Operational Technology (OT) and Industrial Control System (ICS) attacks. Topics include SCADA security, ICS architecture, Stuxnet, supply chain attacks, zero trust architecture, and physical security hacking.

Key Topics Covered

  • ICS/SCADA architecture: PLCs, RTUs, HMI, DCS
  • Stuxnet analysis: first cyber weapon, USB传播, PLC targeting
  • Supply chain attacks: SolarWinds, code repository compromises
  • Zero trust architecture for OT environments
  • Physical security hacking: locks, access control systems

Important Terms & Concepts

Stuxnet: First known cyber weapon targeting industrial SCADA. Spread via USB, exploited 4 zero-days. Targeted Siemens PLCs controlling uranium centrifuges. Discovered 2010.
Zero Trust: Never trust, always verify. Applies to OT/ICS — microsegmentation, least privilege, continuous monitoring for industrial networks.

How to Study This Module

  • Understand ICS/SCADA architecture layers
  • Know Stuxnet attack chain
  • Learn supply chain attack techniques

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes ICS security different from IT?
ICS prioritizes safety and availability over confidentiality. Downtime can be catastrophic (power plants, water treatment). Patching is harder due to 24/7 operation requirements.
