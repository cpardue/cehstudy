Every module page is a condensed study resource: the key topics that surface on the exam, the terms and concepts to know cold, how the material is tested, and a short FAQ. Each module also has its own share of the 306-card flashcard deck and a 10-question practice quiz, so one page gives you notes, cards, and a self-test in one place.

How to Work Through the CEH v13 Modules

The exam is 125 multiple-choice questions in four hours, so breadth beats depth — you need a working answer on every domain, not mastery of one. A sequence that works for most candidates:

  • Pass one — everything, lightly. Read all 20 module pages once, in order, without stopping to drill. The goal is a map of the syllabus and a feel for where you already know the material and where you don't.
  • Pass two — flashcards on the weak spots. Run the flashcard app with only your weakest modules toggled on, and repeat until every card comes back without pausing. The stats panel shows exactly which modules are dragging you down.
  • Pass three — practice questions under pressure. Take each module's 10-question quiz in one sitting, no peeking. Read every explanation — especially the ones you missed — and note which distractors keep tempting you.
  • Loop back. Re-test any module that scores below 80%. In exam week, stick to your lowest modules and card review only — new material makes it worse.

⏱ Time Check

Most candidates need six weeks at 1–2 hours a day, or four weeks at 4+ hours a day. Two focused sessions a day — cards in the morning, one module quiz in the evening — prepares you better for 125 questions across 20 domains than weekend cramming.
