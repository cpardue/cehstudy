About CEHStudy

CEHStudy is a free, independent study resource designed to help cybersecurity students prepare for the Certified Ethical Hacker v13 (CEH v13) exam. Our mission is to make quality exam preparation accessible to everyone — no paywalls, no signup walls, no hidden costs.

Who We Are

CEHStudy was created by cybersecurity professionals with hands-on experience in penetration testing, network security, and information assurance. Our team includes certified security practitioners who have both studied for and passed multiple cybersecurity certifications, and who have taught offensive security concepts to students at various levels.

We built this platform because we believe that quality CEH exam preparation should be free and open to all students, regardless of their financial situation. The certification landscape shouldn't require a $500+ study budget before you can begin preparing.

Our Credentials & Experience

  • Penetration Testing: Hands-on experience conducting authorized security assessments across network, web application, and wireless domains
  • Certification Journey: Team members hold or have studied for certifications including Security+, CEH, OSCP-level practical skills, and cloud security credentials
  • Teaching Experience: Content reviewed against NIST Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF 2.0) and OWASP Top 10 standards to ensure industry alignment
  • Student Perspective: We've been on the other side of the exam, struggling through dense study guides and paying for flashcard apps that could have been free

Content Accuracy & Sources

All CEHStudy content is cross-referenced against authoritative cybersecurity sources to ensure accuracy:

  • EC-Council CEH Official Program — Exam objectives, domains, and curriculum structure
  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF 2.0) — Risk management concepts and security principles
  • OWASP Top 10 — Web application vulnerability classifications used in our content
  • CWE Top 25 — Software weakness classifications referenced in practice questions

Why This Site Exists

  • The CEH v13 exam is one of the most recognized cybersecurity certifications worldwide
  • Premium study materials and flashcard apps often cost $50-$200+
  • Students need reliable, free resources to supplement their training
  • Accessible study tools reduce barriers to entry in cybersecurity careers

How Our Flashcards Were Created

Each flashcard set is developed through a multi-step process:

  • Curriculum mapping: Content is mapped directly to the official CEH v13 curriculum domains
  • Original content creation: All questions, answers, and explanations are written by our team — these are not copied from any commercial source
  • Peer review: Content is reviewed by multiple reviewers with cybersecurity expertise
  • Educational accuracy: We prioritize educational clarity over exam-style mimicry
  • Regular updates: Content is reviewed and updated to reflect current CEH v13 syllabus changes
