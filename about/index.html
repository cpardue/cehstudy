About CEHStudy — Free CEH v13 Study Resources

Free CEH v13 study resources for everyone

CEHStudy provides completely free CEH v13 study resources — flashcards, practice questions, study guides, and more. Our mission is to make CEH exam preparation accessible to everyone, regardless of budget.

About the Author

Chris Pardue is a certified security professional (CEH) with extensive experience in cybersecurity education. He created CEHStudy to help candidates prepare for the CEH v13 exam without the burden of expensive study materials.

Our Free Resources

  • 306 Flashcards: Comprehensive coverage across all 20 CEH v13 modules with progress tracking
  • 200+ Practice Questions: 10 questions per module with detailed explanations for every answer
  • 20 Study Modules: Complete notes for every CEH v13 domain
  • 6-Week Study Plan: Daily-level schedule sequenced by exam weight
  • Glossary: Key CEH terminology with definitions

Our Mission

The CEH exam costs $1,250-$1,450 for a voucher. Study materials shouldn't add to that cost. Every resource on CEHStudy is free, with no signup required and no premium tiers.
