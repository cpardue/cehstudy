About CEHStudy

CEHStudy provides free, accessible study tools for anyone preparing for the EC-Council Certified Ethical Hacker v13 exam.

Who We Are

CEHStudy was created by cybersecurity students who recognized a gap: quality CEH prep materials were either expensive or poorly organized. We built this platform to give everyone free access to structured study resources.

Why We Built This

The CEH v13 exam covers a broad range of topics, from footprinting and scanning to cloud security and post-exploitation. Many students need a flexible way to review key concepts anywhere. Our flashcards deliver exactly that.

How the Flashcards Were Created

All flashcard content is original material written by experienced cybersecurity professionals and educators who have studied the official CEH v13 curriculum. Each card covers a specific concept, term, or technique aligned with the exam domains.

Content Review Process

Our content follows a three-step review process:

  • • Drafted against CEH v13 exam objectives
  • • Peer-reviewed by multiple contributors
  • • Updated regularly based on user feedback
