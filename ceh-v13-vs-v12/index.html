CEH v13 vs v12: Complete Comparison of What Changed in CEH v13 (2026)

Last updated August 2026 · Everything that changed from v12 to v13

Reviewed by the CEHStudy team · Certified security practitioners · Last reviewed August 12, 2026

EC-Council released CEH v13 with significant changes from v12. The exam code remains 312-50, but the content has expanded from ~10 modules to 20 detailed modules. This comparison covers what changed, what's new, and what was removed.

New Modules in CEH v13

  • IoT/OT Security: New domain covering Industrial Control Systems and Internet of Things vulnerabilities
  • Cloud Computing: Dedicated module on cloud security, AWS/Azure/GCP threats
  • Advanced Malware Analysis: Expanded malware content with deep-dive into advanced persistent threats
  • Mobile Platform Security: iOS and Android penetration testing specifics

Frequently Asked Questions

What changed in CEH v13?

CEH v13 expanded from 10 to 20 modules with new content on IoT/OT security, cloud computing, and advanced malware. The exam code remains 312-50.

Is CEH v13 harder than v12?

CEH v13 covers more domains but the passing score remains 60%. The additional content means more study material but also more opportunities to earn points.
