Online vs In-Person Exam

  • Online Proctored: Take from home with webcam monitoring. More flexible scheduling.
  • In-Person: Test center environment with invigilator. Better for those who prefer a controlled setting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions are on the CEH exam?

The CEH v13 exam has 125 multiple-choice questions to be completed in 4 hours.

What is the passing score for CEH?

The passing score is 60% — you need at least 75 correct answers out of 125 questions.
