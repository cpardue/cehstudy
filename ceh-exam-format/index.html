CEH Exam Question Types Explained

The CEH v13 exam features two types of multiple choice questions:

  • Single-answer questions: One correct option among four or five choices. Most straightforward — pick the single best answer.
  • Multiple-answer questions: More than one correct option. These are typically flagged with instructions like "Select TWO that apply" or "Choose all that apply." Missing even one correct option means you lose the entire question.

📌 Pro Tip — Identifying Multiple-Answer Questions

EC-Council always signals multiple-answer questions explicitly. Look for phrases like "Select TWO that apply," "Which THREE of the following...," or "Choose all correct answers." If you see these instructions, budget extra time — each option must be evaluated independently.

CEH Exam Duration and Time Management

Four hours for 125 questions gives you an average of 1 minute 53 seconds per question. However, some questions take 30 seconds while others (especially scenario-based questions with long preamble text) can consume 3–4 minutes. Effective time management is critical:

  • First pass strategy: Answer every question you know confidently in under 90 seconds, flag the rest
  • Second pass: Return to flagged questions with remaining time
  • Final 10 minutes: No new answers — use this time to review flags and confirm no blanks

How to Register and Schedule the CEH Exam

  1. Create an EC-Council account at eccouncil.org (required for member pricing)
  2. Purchase your exam voucher through the EC-Council member portal or directly from EC-Council
  3. Receive your voucher code via email (usually instant for online purchases)
  4. Schedule your exam at a Pearson VUE test center or select online proctored delivery
  5. Bring valid ID to the test center (or ensure your webcam/environment meets online proctoring requirements)

CEH Exam Delivery Options

Pearson VUE Test Center

  • Take the exam at a physical testing center near you
  • Professional environment with dedicated testing facilities
  • Arrive 30 minutes early for check-in procedures
  • Personal ID verification required

Online Proctored (Remote)

  • Take the exam from home or office via remote proctoring
  • Requires a quiet, private space with a stable internet connection
  • Webcam, microphone, and screen sharing must be enabled throughout the exam
  • Run the system check at least 24 hours before your scheduled exam
  • Your desk and camera view will be monitored continuously

CEH Exam Retake Policy

If you do not pass the CEH exam on your first attempt:

  • Waiting period: 30 days before you can retake the exam
  • New voucher required: You must purchase a fresh voucher for each attempt
  • Results available immediately: Your pass/fail status is displayed at the end of the exam
  • No limit on attempts: You can retake as many times as needed, subject to the 30-day waiting period and voucher purchase

Frequently Asked Questions About the CEH Exam Format

How many questions are on the CEH v13 exam?

The CEH v13 (312-50) exam has 125 multiple choice questions to be completed in 4 hours. Questions include both single-answer and multiple-answer types.

What is the passing score for the CEH exam?

The passing score for the CEH v13 exam is 60% — you need at least 75 correct answers out of 125.

How much does the CEH v13 exam cost?

The exam voucher costs $1,250 USD for EC-Council members and $1,450 for non-members. The voucher is valid for 6 months and covers one attempt at the 125-question exam via Pearson VUE.

Can I retake the CEH exam if I fail?

Yes. You can retake the CEH exam after 30 days by purchasing a new voucher. Results are available immediately at the end of the exam.

What question types are on the CEH exam?

The CEH v13 exam includes both single-answer and multiple-answer multiple choice questions. Single-answer questions have one correct option; multiple-answer questions may have more than one correct answer.
