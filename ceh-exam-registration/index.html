How to Register for CEH v13 Exam: Complete Step-by-Step Guide 2026

Last updated August 2026 · From account creation to exam day

Reviewed by the CEHStudy team · Certified security practitioners · Last reviewed August 12, 2026

This step-by-step guide covers everything you need to know about registering for the CEH v13 (312-50) exam: creating your EC-Council account, purchasing a voucher ($1,250 member / $1,450 non-member), scheduling through Pearson VUE (test center or online proctored), and understanding retake policies. Follow these steps to go from zero to exam day with confidence.

Step 1: Create Your EC-Council Account

  1. Visit eccouncil.org
  2. Click "Sign Up" and create your account
  3. Verify your email address
  4. Complete your profile with accurate personal information

💡 Pro Tip — Member vs Non-Member

If you plan to take multiple certifications, EC-Council membership saves $200 on the voucher. Annual membership is ~$195, so it pays for itself after one exam.

Step 2: Purchase Your Exam Voucher

OptionCost
EC-Council Member$1,250
Non-Member$1,450

The voucher is valid for 6 months from purchase and covers one exam attempt. Purchase through your EC-Council member portal.

Step 3: Schedule Your Exam via Pearson VUE

  1. Visit Pearson VUE EC-Council page
  2. Enter your voucher code
  3. Choose test center or online proctored
  4. Select your preferred date and time

Step 4: Exam Day Preparation

For test centers: arrive 30 minutes early with valid ID. For online proctored: run system check 24 hours before, ensure clean desk and stable internet.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the CEH exam cost?

The CEH v13 exam voucher costs $1,250 for EC-Council members and $1,450 for non-members. The voucher is valid for 6 months from purchase date.

How do I schedule my CEH exam?

After purchasing your voucher, schedule through Pearson VUE. You can choose between a test center or online proctored delivery.

What is the CEH exam retake policy?

If you fail the CEH exam, you can retake it after a 30-day waiting period by purchasing a new voucher. Results are available immediately at the end of the exam.
