How to Register for CEH v13 Exam: Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

Last updated August 2026 · Complete registration guide

Reviewed by the CEHStudy team · Certified security practitioners · Last reviewed August 12, 2026

This step-by-step guide walks you through registering for the CEH v13 exam: creating your EC-Council account, purchasing a voucher, scheduling through Pearson VUE, and what to expect on exam day.

Step 1: Create an EC-Council Account

Visit the EC-Council website and create your account. You will use this account to purchase vouchers and track your certification progress.

Step 2: Purchase Your Exam Voucher

The exam voucher costs $1,250 for EC-Council members or $1,450 for non-members. The voucher is valid for 6 months from purchase.

Step 3: Schedule Through Pearson VUE

Visit the Pearson VUE website, select CEH v13 (312-50), and choose your exam date, time, and location. You can take the exam at a testing center or online.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the CEH exam cost?

$1,250 for EC-Council members, $1,450 for non-members. Valid for 6 months.

How do I register for the CEH exam?

Create an EC-Council account, purchase your voucher, then schedule through Pearson VUE.
