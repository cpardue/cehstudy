How to Register for the CEH v13 Exam — Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Registering for the EC-Council Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH v13) exam is a straightforward process, but knowing exactly what to expect saves time and avoids common mistakes. This guide walks you through every step from creating your EC-Council account to receiving your exam results.

Step-by-Step Registration Process

1

Create an EC-Council Member Account

Visit the official EC-Council website and create a free member account. You'll need a valid email address and basic personal information. This account is required to purchase exam vouchers, access member resources, and track your certification status.

Tip: EC-Council members receive a discounted exam voucher price ($1,250 vs $1,450) and access to the member portal with additional study resources.

2

Purchase Your CEH Exam Voucher

Once your account is set up, purchase your CEH v13 exam voucher directly from EC-Council. The voucher is valid for 6 months from the date of purchase — plan your study timeline accordingly.

Exam Cost Breakdown:
• EC-Council Member: $1,250 USD
• Non-Member: $1,450 USD
• Exam Rescheduling (if needed): $95 USD
• Annual Membership Renewal: ~$195 USD/year

Some employers, universities, or government agencies may sponsor your exam cost. Check if your organization offers a certification reimbursement program before purchasing.

3

Schedule Your Exam via Pearson VUE

Use your voucher code to schedule the exam through Pearson VUE, EC-Council's designated testing provider. You have two options:

  • Test Center: Visit a physical Pearson VUE testing location. Search for centers near you on the Pearson VUE website.
  • Online Proctored: Take the exam from home using a secure browser with webcam monitoring. Requires a quiet room, stable internet (5+ Mbps), and a valid government ID.

Scheduling tips:

  • Book 4-8 weeks out to allow adequate preparation time
  • Avoid scheduling during peak exam periods (Monday-Friday mornings tend to have fewer availability slots)
  • If taking online proctored, complete the system check at least 24 hours before your exam
4

Prepare for the Exam

Use this preparation window to review all 20 CEH v13 domains. Our recommended approach:

  1. Follow our 6-week study plan for structured weekly progression
  2. Practice with free CEH practice questions to test knowledge retention
  3. Review all 20 module flashcards to cover every domain
  4. Take timed mock exams under exam conditions (4 hours, 125 questions)

What the exam covers: The CEH v13 blueprint weights domains differently — Scanning & Vulnerability Assessment (20%), Footprinting & Reconnaissance (15%), Exploiting System Services (15%) represent the highest-weighted areas. Prioritize these during your study period.

5

Exam Day & Receiving Results

If taking at a test center: Arrive 30 minutes early with two forms of ID (one government-issued photo ID + one supporting ID). No personal items allowed in the testing room.

If taking online proctored: Complete the system check, verify your environment meets requirements, and have your ID ready. The proctor will verify your identity via video before the exam begins.

Results: Your score is available immediately upon completing the exam. A passing score of 60% or higher (75+ correct answers) grants CEH certification. If you don't pass, you can retake the exam after 30 days with a new voucher.

Frequently Asked Questions About CEH Exam Registration

Q: Do I need a specific educational background to take the CEH exam?
A: No. The CEH v13 exam has no formal prerequisites. You do not need a degree in computer science, prior security certifications, or work experience. Many candidates self-study for 4-8 weeks before testing.

Q: Can I take the CEH exam without completing an EC-Council training course?
A: Yes. While EC-Council offers official training courses (CEH v13 course with instructor), self-study is completely acceptable and often preferred by experienced professionals. The exam tests your knowledge regardless of how you studied.

Q: What happens if I fail the CEH exam?
A: You'll receive your score immediately but will not be certified. You can retake the exam after 30 days by purchasing a new voucher. Most candidates who retake with adequate additional preparation pass on their second attempt.

Q: Is the CEH exam harder than Security+ or PenTest+?
A: CEH is generally considered intermediate-level difficulty — harder than CompTIA Security+ but comparable to CompTIA PenTest+. The breadth of topics (20 domains) is what makes it challenging rather than the depth of individual questions.
