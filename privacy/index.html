Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This policy explains what cehstudy.com does and does not do with your information. In short: there are no accounts, we never ask for personal details, and the only third-party technologies on this site are Google's advertising services, disclosed in full below.

Information We Do Not Collect

CEHStudy is a free study resource. You do not need an account, an email address, or any other personal information to use the flashcards, practice questions, module notes, or glossary. We do not collect names or contact details from visitors, and we do not sell or share visitor information with anyone.

Data Stored in Your Browser

The flashcard app keeps your study progress in your browser's local storage — which cards you have reviewed, where you stopped in a deck, and per-module progress counts. This data:

  • Stays on your own device and is never sent to any server
  • Is used only to restore your position when you return to a page
  • Can be removed at any time by clearing your browser's site data

Advertising and Cookies (Google AdSense)

This website uses Google AdSense, an online advertising service provided by Google Inc. ("Google"). We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on this site.

Google and its third-party partners use cookies and tracking technologies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings page at google.com/settings/ads, where you can also manage cookies. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

Search Console

We use Google Search Console to monitor how this site is indexed and how it performs in Google search results. Google Search Console does not set cookies on this site and does not collect personal information about visitors to CEHStudy.

Other Analytics and Tracking

Apart from the Google AdSense advertising technologies described above, we do not use Google Analytics or any other third-party analytics, tracking pixels, or remarketing scripts on this site. The only analytics tools in use are Google AdSense and Google Search Console.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Open an issue in the CEHStudy GitHub repository — github.com/cpardue/cehstudy, Issues — or use our contact page. We typically respond within 48 hours.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how this site collects or uses information — including adding new analytics or advertising technologies — we will update this page and its last-updated date before the change takes effect.
