Is CEH Worth It in 2026? Honest Pros & Cons Analysis

Last updated August 2026 · Honest analysis

Reviewed by the CEHStudy team · Certified security practitioners · Last reviewed August 12, 2026

Is the CEH v13 certification worth it in 2026? Here's an honest analysis of pros and cons to help you decide.

Pros of CEH Certification

  • Industry Recognition: CEH is widely recognized by employers and government agencies
  • Salary Impact: 10-25% salary increase on average
  • Government Compliance: Required for many DoD 8570/8140 positions
  • Career Advancement: Opens doors to pentesting and security analyst roles

Cons of CEH Certification

  • Cost: $1,250-$1,450 for the exam voucher
  • Multiple-Choice Only: No hands-on practical component in the exam
  • Alternatives Exist: OSCP offers more technical validation for pentesting

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CEH worth it in 2026?

Yes for career changers and government/defense roles. Consider alternatives like OSCP for pure technical penetration testing paths.

How much does CEH increase salary?

CEH certified professionals typically earn 10-25% more than non-certified peers, depending on role and experience.
