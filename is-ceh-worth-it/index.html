Is CEH Worth It in 2026? Salary Data, Job Market & ROI Analysis

The EC-Council Certified Ethical Hacker certification costs $1,250-$1,450 for an exam voucher, but CEH-certified professionals earn $85,000-$130,000+ annually. With over 30,000 open cybersecurity positions in the US alone and CEH recognized in 180+ countries, the return on investment is strong — especially when you factor in free study resources like ours.
