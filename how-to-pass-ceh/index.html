How to Pass CEH v13 Exam: 7 Proven Strategies (2026)

Last updated August 2026 · 7 strategies that work

Reviewed by the CEHStudy team · Certified security practitioners · Last reviewed August 12, 2026

Passing the CEH v13 exam requires a strategic approach. Here are 7 proven strategies to help you pass on your first attempt.

Strategy 1: Follow a Structured Study Plan

Use our free 6-week study plan that sequences modules by exam weight. Start with high-weight domains for maximum point yield.

Strategy 2: Master the Flashcards

Complete all 306 flashcards at least twice. Focus on cards you struggle with using spaced repetition.

Strategy 3: Practice with Questions

Work through all 200+ practice questions and understand the explanation for each answer.

Strategy 4: Hands-On Labs

Practice with Kali Linux tools — Nmap, Wireshark, Burp Suite. Even though CEH is multiple-choice, practical experience reinforces concepts.

Strategy 5: Focus on Weak Areas

Use your progress tracking to identify weak areas and revisit those modules before the exam.

Strategy 6: Understand, Don't Memorize

CEH v13 tests application of knowledge. Focus on understanding concepts rather than memorizing answers.

Strategy 7: Take Practice Exams

Simulate exam conditions with timed practice sessions. Aim for 80%+ before scheduling your real exam.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to pass CEH exam?

Use our 6-week study plan, complete all flashcards, practice with 200+ questions, and focus on high-weight domains first.

What is the passing score for CEH?

The passing score is 60% — you need at least 75 correct answers out of 125 questions.
