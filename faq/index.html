General CEH Certification Questions

What is CEH v13 (Certified Ethical Hacker) certification?

CEH v13 (Certified Ethical Hacker version 13) is the latest version of EC-Council's Certified Ethical Hacker certification. It covers 20 domains of ethical hacking including footprinting, scanning, vulnerability analysis, system hacking, malware, sniffing, social engineering, IoT/OT hacking, cloud security, and lateral movement. The exam code is 312-50 with 125 multiple choice questions over 4 hours. CEH is one of the most recognized ethical hacking certifications globally and is approved by the U.S. Department of Defense.

Is CEH worth getting in 2026?

Absolutely. CEH-certified professionals earn $85,000-$130,000+ annually depending on experience and location. CEH is approved by the U.S. Department of Defense under DoD Directive 8570.01M and recognized in 180+ countries worldwide. It remains one of the most valuable entry-level cybersecurity certifications for penetration testing, ethical hacking, and security consulting careers.

What is the difference between CEH v12 and CEH v13?

CEH v13 introduces new topics like IoT/OT hacking, cloud computing security, lateral movement, and advanced exploitation techniques while removing some legacy content from v12. The exam code changed from 312-00 to 312-50. If you're switching from v12 to v13, focus on the new domains which include modern attack vectors like cloud-based attacks and IoT penetration testing.

Do I need experience before taking the CEH exam?

EC-Council requires 2 years of work experience in IT security OR an official EC-Council training certification (CET) to sit for the exam. If you don't have the required experience, you can enroll in EC-Council's training program which includes the exam voucher. The CET option is the most common path for new candidates.

How do I register for the CEH exam?

You can register through EC-Council's website or via Pearson VUE test centers worldwide. You'll need to either enroll in EC-Council training or prove your 2 years of IT security experience. Visit ec-council.org for registration details and find a Pearson VUE test center near you.

CEH Exam Details & Preparation

What is the passing score for the CEH v13 exam?

The passing score for the CEH v13 exam (312-50) is 60%, which means you need to answer at least 75 out of 125 questions correctly. The exam has 125 multiple choice questions and lasts 4 hours. Questions cover all 20 domains with varying weights — Scanning (20%), Footprinting (15%), System Hacking (15%), and Web Application Attacks (15%) are the highest-weighted areas.

How long should I study for the CEH exam?

Most candidates spend 4–8 weeks preparing, studying 10–15 hours per week using our free CEH study plan. If you already have security experience (Security+, CISSP, or hands-on penetration testing), you may need less time — as little as 2-3 weeks for a focused CEH exam cram. Complete beginners should plan for the full 6–8 weeks with consistent daily practice.

What is the cost of the CEH v13 exam?

The standalone CEH exam voucher costs $1,199 USD. If you enroll in EC-Council's official training program (CET), the exam is included for $849. Third-party vouchers may be available through authorized resellers at discounted rates. Check the official EC-Council website for current pricing and registration options.

How hard is the CEH exam?

The CEH v13 exam is moderately difficult. Candidates with IT/security experience typically find it manageable with 4-6 weeks of study. Beginners should plan 6-8 weeks using our free CEH study plan. The key challenge is breadth — the exam covers 20 domains, so consistent daily practice across all topics matters more than deep mastery of any single area. Overall first-attempt pass rate is approximately 65-70%. Focus your study on Footprinting and Scanning — together they account for 35% of exam questions.

What is on the CEH v13 exam?

The CEH v13 exam (312-50) covers 20 domains: Footprinting & Reconnaissance (15%), Scanning Vulnerabilities (20%), System Hacking (15%), Malware (10%), Sniffing (5%), Social Engineering (10%), DoS (5%), Session Hijacking (5%), Evading IDS (5%), Web Server Hacking (5%), Web Application Attacks (10%), SQL Injection (10%), Wireless Networks (5%), Mobile Platforms (5%), IoT/OT (5%), Cloud Computing (5%), Cryptography (10%), Lateral Movement (5%), and Post-Exploitation (10%). The exam consists of 125 multiple choice questions, lasts 4 hours, and requires a 60% score to pass. Use our 20 flashcard modules to systematically cover every domain.

How many questions are on the CEH v13 exam?

The CEH v13 exam (code 312-50) has exactly 125 multiple choice questions to be completed in 4 hours. You must answer at least 75 correctly (60%) to pass. There are no unscored pilot questions, no hands-on labs, and no fill-in-the-blank items — only single-answer multiple choice covering all 20 CEH domains. This means you get approximately 1.9 minutes per question on average.

CEH Career & Salary Questions

What jobs can I get with CEH certification?

CEH certification opens doors to roles like Penetration Tester, Ethical Hacker, Security Consultant, SOC Analyst, Incident Responder, Vulnerability Assessor, and Security Engineer. Many employers specifically list CEH as a preferred or required qualification for offensive security positions.

How much do CEH-certified professionals earn?

CEH-certified professionals earn $85,000-$130,000+ annually depending on experience level, location, and specialization. Entry-level penetration testers typically earn $70K-$90K, mid-level professionals earn $90K-$120K, and senior ethical hackers or security consultants can earn $120K-$160K+.

CEH vs Security+ — which should I get first?

CompTIA Security+ is often recommended as a first certification because it covers broader cybersecurity fundamentals. CEH builds on that foundation with hands-on offensive security and penetration testing skills. If you're new to cybersecurity, start with Security+. If you already have IT experience and want to specialize in ethical hacking, go straight to CEH.
