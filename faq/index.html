General CEH Certification Questions

What is CEH / CEH v13?

Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH) is a certification offered by EC-Council that validates your ability to think like a hacker — to identify vulnerabilities in IT systems before malicious actors can exploit them. CEH v13 (exam code 312-50) is the latest version, updated with modern attack vectors including IoT/OT hacking, cloud security, and lateral movement.

Is CEH worth it in 2026?

Yes. CEH certification opens doors to penetration testing, ethical hacking, and cybersecurity careers with strong salary expectations ($85K-$130K+ annually) and job opportunities. CEH is recognized in 180+ countries and trusted by Fortune 500 companies.

CEH Exam Details

How many questions in the CEH exam?

The CEH v13 exam has 125 multiple choice questions to be completed in 4 hours with a passing score of 60%.

How hard is the CEH exam?

Difficulty varies by background. Candidates with security experience find it manageable; beginners should plan 4-8 weeks of study using our free CEH study plan.

How long does it take to prepare for the CEH exam?

Most candidates spend 4–8 weeks preparing, studying 10–15 hours per week using our free CEH study plan. If you already have security experience (Security+, CISSP, or hands-on penetration testing), you may need less time — as little as 2-3 weeks for a focused CEH exam cram. Complete beginners should plan for the full 6–8 weeks.

Can I study for CEH in 30 days?

Yes, a focused 30-day study plan is possible if you can dedicate 2-3 hours daily. Use our free 30-day CEH study plan with hands-on labs built in. Candidates with existing security experience are best suited for this timeline.

What is the cost of the CEH exam?

The standalone exam voucher costs $1,199 USD. If you enroll in EC-Council's official training (CET), the exam is included for $849. Third-party vouchers may be available through authorized resellers at discounted rates.

Where can I take the CEH exam?

The CEH exam is available at Pearson VUE test centers worldwide or you can take it online with a remote proctor.

What are the CEH exam prerequisites?

You need either 2 years of work experience in IT security OR an EC-Council authorized training certification. Both paths allow you to register for the 312-50 exam.

How long is the CEH certification valid?

The CEH certification is valid for 3 years. You can renew it through continuing education credits or by passing a higher-level EC-Council certification.

Can I retake the CEH exam if I fail?

Yes, you can retake the CEH exam. EC-Council offers free retests for first-time failures with their official training, and voucher holders can retake after a 30-day waiting period.

CEH vs Other Certifications

CEH vs Security+ which is better?

CEH focuses on offensive security while Security+ covers broader fundamentals. Both are valuable for cybersecurity careers.

What is the difference between CEH and PenTest+?

CEH has broader domain coverage including IoT/OT hacking, cloud computing security, mobile platform attacks, and cryptography. PenTest+ is more focused purely on penetration testing methodology. Choose CEV for broader career flexibility.

CEH Exam Topics

What are the CEH exam domains?

The CEH v13 exam blueprint covers Footprinting & Reconnaissance (15%), Scanning Vulnerabilities (20%), Exploiting System Services (15%), Evading IDS/Firewalls (5%), Hacking Mobile Platforms (5%), IoT/OT (5%), Cloud Computing (5%), Social Engineering (10%), and more.

What is the latest CEH version?

CEH v13 is the current version, updated with modern attack vectors including lateral movement, cloud security, and IoT/OT hacking.

What is the difference between CEH v12 and v13?

CEH v13 adds new domains including IoT/OT hacking, cloud computing security, and lateral movement while removing some legacy content. It also includes updated attack vectors reflecting modern threat landscapes.

What are common CEH exam topics?

High-yield topics include footprinting, scanning, vulnerability analysis, system hacking, malware threats, social engineering, web application attacks, SQL injection, wireless security, cryptography, and cloud computing security.

What tools do I need to know for the CEH exam?

Key tools include Nmap, Metasploit, Wireshark, Burp Suite, Nessus, Nikto, Hydra, John the Ripper, Aircrack-ng, Kali Linux, and SQLmap.

How many ports should I memorize for the CEH exam?

Memorize at least 15 common ports: 21 FTP, 22 SSH, 23 Telnet, 25 SMTP, 53 DNS, 80 HTTP, 110 POP3, 143 IMAP, 443 HTTPS, 445 SMB, 993 IMAPS, 995 POP3S, 3306 MySQL, 3389 RDP, and 8080 HTTP-Proxy.

What are the most important Nmap scan types for the CEH exam?

The most tested Nmap scans are: SYN stealth (-sS), TCP connect (-sT), FIN scan (-sF), XMAS scan (-sX), NULL scan (-sN), version detection (-sV), and OS detection (-O). Master these for the exam.

What is the TCP three-way handshake?

The TCP three-way handshake consists of: SYN (synchronize) → SYN-ACK (synchronize-acknowledge) → ACK (acknowledge). This is how a TCP connection is established and is fundamental to understanding network scanning.

What are the types of XSS attacks?

There are three types of XSS: Reflected XSS (one-time via URL), Stored XSS (persisted in database, served to multiple users), and DOM-based XSS (client-side only, no server interaction).

What are the types of SQL injection?

The four main types are: Union-based (uses UNION SELECT statement), Boolean-based blind (true/false responses), Time-based blind (SLEEP/BENCHMARK functions), and Error-based (database errors revealed in response).
