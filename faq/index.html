This FAQ covers the most common questions about the CEH v13 (312-50) exam: pricing, registration, study time, passing score, retake policy, and more. If you don't see your question here, reach out via the contact page.

CEH Exam Overview

What is the CEH v13 exam?

The CEH v13 (exam code 312-50) is EC-Council's Certified Ethical Hacker certification. It covers 20 domains including Footprinting, Scanning, System Hacking, Malware Threats, Social Engineering, and more. The exam has 125 multiple-choice questions to be completed in 4 hours.

What is the passing score for CEH v13?

The passing score is 60% — you need at least 75 correct answers out of 125 questions. Results are available immediately at the end of the exam.

CEH Exam Cost & Registration

How much does the CEH exam cost?

The exam voucher costs $1,250 USD for EC-Council members and $1,450 for non-members. The voucher is valid for 6 months from purchase date.

How do I register for the CEH exam?

Create an EC-Council account, purchase your voucher, then schedule through Pearson VUE.

CEH Study & Preparation

How long to prepare for CEH?

Most candidates need 6 weeks at 1-2 hours daily or 4 weeks with 4+ hours daily. Experience with Security+ or hands-on IT can reduce prep time.

Can I pass CEH with free resources only?

Yes. Our free flashcards (306 cards) and practice questions (200+) cover all 20 exam domains. Hands-on labs are recommended but not required for the multiple-choice exam.

CEH Exam Retake Policy

What is the CEH retake policy?

If you fail, you can retake after 30 days by purchasing a new voucher. Results are available immediately at exam end.
