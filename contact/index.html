Contact CEHStudy

We welcome your feedback, error reports, and suggestions. Our flashcards are continuously improved thanks to community input. Every correction helps make this resource better for all students.

How to Reach Us

Email: contact@cehstudy.com

We aim to respond to all messages within 48 hours.

Reporting Errors

If you find an error in any flashcard, please include the following in your message:

  • The module number and card content (question and answer)
  • A clear explanation of what is incorrect
  • The correct information with a source if possible

Suggesting New Content

Have ideas for new flashcards, study guides, or features? We'd love to hear them. Let us know what would help you study more effectively.

Volunteering to Contribute

If you're a cybersecurity professional who wants to help review or create content, we welcome volunteers. Get in touch and let us know how you can contribute.
