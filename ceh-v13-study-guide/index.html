Study Tips for CEH v13

  • Study by weight: Focus on high-weight domains first for maximum ROI
  • Use free flashcards: Review 15-20 cards daily with our 306-card deck
  • Practice questions: Complete all 200+ questions with explanations in our free bank
  • Hands-on labs: Practice Nmap, Metasploit, Wireshark, Burp Suite
  • Timed mocks: Take at least 2 timed mock exams scoring 80%+

Last-Minute Review Checklist

  • Review all flashcards with errors
  • Re-read practice question explanations for weak modules
  • Memorize key port numbers, scan types, and tool commands
  • Understand TCP handshake, common protocols
  • Get adequate sleep the night before

Frequently Asked Questions

How to pass CEH v13 exam first time?

Study the high-weight domains first (Scanning 20%, Footprinting 15%), use our free flashcards daily, complete all practice questions with explanations, and take at least 2 timed mock exams scoring 80%+. Budget ~200 hours of study over 6 weeks.

What is the passing score for CEH v13?

The passing score for CEH v13 (312-50) is 60% — 75 correct answers out of 125 questions.

How long to prepare for CEH v13?

Most candidates need 6 weeks at 1-2 hours daily, or 4 weeks with 4+ hours daily. Experience with Security+ or hands-on IT can reduce prep time.
